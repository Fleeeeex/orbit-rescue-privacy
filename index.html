Orbit Rescue – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Orbit Rescue is a game made by one person, published as Fleeeeex ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and what you can do about it. If you have any question, write to xilef.jan@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You can play without an account. Your progress is then stored only on your phone.
  • With an account, your progress is saved online so it is not lost, and your best score can appear on the leaderboards with the name you chose and your country.
  • The free version shows ads. You choose whether the ads may fit your interests; you can change this anytime in the settings.
  • Buying "Remove ads" goes through Google Play or the App Store; I never see your payment details.
  • You can delete your account and its online data from inside the game, or by email (see Delete your account).

1. Information the game uses

Game progress and settings. Your scores, achievements, unlocked and selected skins, tutorial progress and settings (sound, vibration, language) are stored on your phone. If you have an account, a copy is stored online so it can be restored on a new phone.

Account. On Android the game signs you in with Google Play Games, on iPhone with Game Center. This gives the game an anonymous player ID; the game does not receive your email address or your Google / Apple password. You can also play without an account.

Leaderboard name and country. If you pick a name, it is shown on the leaderboards next to your best score and your country. Names are checked so no two players share one. Your country is found automatically from your phone's SIM card or App Store account country, or, if those are not available, from your internet connection's location (your IP address is sent once to a country lookup service, country.is or Cloudflare, which only return a country code). Your country is only used for the country leaderboards.

Ads. The free version shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Ads and its ad partners). To show and count ads, these services receive information such as your device's advertising ID, device type, operating system, IP address and how you interact with the ads. After your first game you are asked whether ads may fit your interests (personalized ads). If you say no, only general ads are shown. On iPhone, the system also asks whether the app may track you; ads are not personalized if you decline. You can change your choice in the game's settings ("Personalized ads") and in your phone's settings.

Purchases. "Remove ads" is bought through Google Play or the App Store, which handle the payment. The game receives a receipt of the purchase, which my server checks with the store so the purchase belongs to your account and your name can be shown in gold on the leaderboards. For a few months after the purchase, my server also asks the store now and then whether it was refunded; a refunded purchase no longer removes ads.

Not collected. The game does not collect your real name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or microphone.

2. Services that process the information

  • Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies): accounts, online save, leaderboards and the game's server code – Unity Privacy Policy.
  • Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) and the ad networks they work with: ads – Unity Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Games and Google Play (Google): sign-in and purchases on Android – Google Privacy Policy.
  • Game Center and the App Store (Apple): sign-in and purchases on iPhone – Apple Privacy Policy.
  • country.is / Cloudflare: the country lookup described above.

These services may process data outside your country, under their own safeguards. I do not sell your information.

3. Why (legal basis)

Your progress, account, leaderboards and purchases are needed to provide the game you asked for. Personalized ads are only shown with your consent. General ads and keeping the game working and fair (for example checking names and purchases) are based on my legitimate interest in offering the game for free.

4. How long

Progress on your phone stays until you delete the game. Online data (account, save, name, scores, purchase record) stays until you delete your account. Ad services keep their data according to their own policies.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the game, Settings → account button → "Delete account". This removes your name, your leaderboard scores and your online save.
  • Personalized ads: switch them off anytime in Settings → "Personalized ads", or reset / limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • You can ask me for a copy of your data, to correct it or to delete it, by writing to xilef.jan@gmail.com. If you live in the EU, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Delete your Orbit Rescue account

You can delete your Orbit Rescue account (developer: Fleeeeex) and its online data in two ways:

  • In the game: open Settings (gear icon) → the account button in the top right corner → "Delete account" → confirm.
  • By email, without the game: write to xilef.jan@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Orbit Rescue account" and your leaderboard name (if you never picked one, write anyway and I will help you find your account). Your account is deleted within 30 days and you get a confirmation.

What is deleted: your account and player ID, your leaderboard name, your scores on all leaderboards, your online save (progress, achievements, skins, settings) and your country.

What is kept: only an anonymous record that a "Remove ads" purchase was already used (a scrambled code made from the store's transaction number, with no name or other personal details), so the same purchase cannot be claimed twice. Your purchase itself stays in your Google Play / App Store account and can be restored after you make a new account. Progress stored on your phone stays until you delete the game.

6. Children

Orbit Rescue is suitable for everyone, but it is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them. Ads for apps and games rated 13+ are blocked in the game. If you believe a child gave the game personal information, write to me and I will delete it.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
